\title{Structural Reasoning for Market Microstructure: Detecting Persistent Dealer Gamma Regimes in the 0DTE Era}

% Author information removed for blind review
% See TitlePage.tex for complete author information

\begin{abstract}
We introduce a structural reasoning framework using large language models to detect emergent coordination patterns in equity index options markets. Analyzing 1,412 30-day GEX sequences (2020--2025), we identify a fundamental regime transition: dealer positioning evolved from exceptional (12\% detection in 2020) to persistent (100\% in 2024), coinciding with 0DTE proliferation. Our framework achieves high-fidelity discrimination that arithmetic thresholds miss, validated through negative controls and temporal obfuscation protocols. We propose a ``scar tissue'' mechanism---concentrated intraday hedging leaving residual overnight inventory---confirmed by a ``great divergence'' in SPY returns: overnight (+21.2\%) vastly outperforming intraday (+0.6\%) since 2023. This demonstrates how structural reasoning architectures can identify market microstructure evolution invisible to traditional econometric approaches.
\end{abstract}
