Dear Graduate College Committee,

I am writing to support Christopher Regan's application for the Graduate College Research Travel Award to attend the 22nd IFIP International Conference on Artificial Intelligence Applications and Innovations (AIAI 2026), to be held July 16--19, 2026, in Chania, Crete, Greece. Mr. Regan will participate virtually.

Mr. Regan is a doctoral candidate in the College of Computing and Software Engineering, working under my supervision. His research focuses on validating large language model (LLM) structural reasoning capabilities through a novel methodology called temporal obfuscation testing. This work addresses a critical challenge in the deployment of AI systems: distinguishing genuine reasoning from training data memorization.

The paper submitted to AIAI 2026, titled "Validating LLM Structural Reasoning: Detecting Persistent Market Regimes Through Temporal Obfuscation," presents a rigorous five-phase validation framework encompassing over 2,200 evaluations across six years of financial market data. The research demonstrates that LLMs can perform genuine structural reasoning when identifying information is stripped from inputs, achieving strong discrimination (69.1 percentage points, p < 0.0001) between distinct market regimes with zero false positives on synthetic controls.

This research constitutes a core component of Mr. Regan's dissertation and represents significant original contributions to the fields of AI validation and applied machine learning. The temporal obfuscation methodology developed in this work is generalizable beyond finance to any domain where LLM memorization threatens evaluation validity.

AIAI 2026, organized by IFIP (International Federation for Information Processing), publishes proceedings in Springer's Lecture Notes in Computer Science series. Presenting at this venue will provide Mr. Regan with valuable peer feedback from the international AI research community and result in a high-quality publication that strengthens his dissertation and academic record.

I strongly recommend Mr. Regan for this award. The conference participation will directly contribute to the successful completion of his doctoral degree and advance his development as an independent researcher.
