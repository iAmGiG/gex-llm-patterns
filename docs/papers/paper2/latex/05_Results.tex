The 39 percentage point persistence gap and \$6.84B magnitude gap demonstrated meaningful discrimination between persistent regimes and transitional periods, supporting proceeding to Phase 2 negative controls despite higher-than-expected detection rate.

\textbf{Confidence Distribution}:
\begin{itemize}
\item 90-100\%: 144 windows (79.6\% of detections)
\item 70-89\%: 37 windows (20.4\% of detections)
\end{itemize}

The 81.2\% detection rate initially raised concerns about framework selectivity (expected 30-50\%). However, two factors supported validity:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Perfect regime homogeneity}: 100\% of detections were persistent\_negative (no false positives on wrong regime type)
\item \textbf{Phase 2 validation}: 0\% FP on transitional/low-magnitude tests proved framework discrimination
\end{enumerate}

Critical question: Is 81.2\% detection evidence of loose criteria, or genuine 2024 market anomaly?

\subsection{Phase 4: 2020 Comparison (Market Structure Evolution Test)}

Phase 4 tested 223 windows from 2020 using identical methodology to establish a pre-0DTE baseline. Results revealed a 69.1 percentage point detection difference between periods.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Phase 4 Detection Results (2020 vs 2024 Comparison)}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{2024} & \textbf{2020} & \textbf{Difference} \\
\midrule
Detection Rate & 81.2\% (181/223) & 12.1\% (27/223) & +69.1 pp \\
Avg Confidence & 86.8\% & 72.4\% & +14.4 pts \\
Regime Type & Negative & Positive & Sign flip \\
Avg Persistence & 96.0\% & 83.3\% & +12.7 pp \\
Avg Magnitude & \$13.95B & \$2.85B & +\$11.10B \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Key Findings}:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Framework is selective}: 2020 detection rate (12.1\%) validates that criteria correctly reject non-regimes when market structure lacks persistence
\item \textbf{2024 exhibits structural anomaly}: 81.2\% detection reflects genuine regime persistence, not methodology artifact
\item \textbf{Regime persistence shift}: 69.1 percentage point difference demonstrates fundamental market structure change between periods
\item \textbf{Regime sign flip}: 2020 predominantly positive GEX (dealer long gamma), 2024 exclusively negative GEX (dealer short gamma)
\item \textbf{Magnitude increase}: 2024 average GEX magnitude 4.9x larger than 2020 (\$13.95B vs \$2.85B)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Combined Interpretation}

The four-phase validation reveals a coherent story:

\textbf{Phase 1 (Q1 2024)}: 71.2\% detection, borderline high but within extreme market bounds

\textbf{Phase 2 (Negative Controls)}: Framework discrimination validated (0\% FP on transitional/low-magnitude, 5x difference on shuffle)

\textbf{Phase 3 (Full 2024)}: 81.2\% detection, initially concerning but consistent with Q1 trend

\textbf{Phase 4 (2020 Comparison)}: 12.1\% detection, proving framework selectivity and confirming 2024 anomaly

\textbf{Conclusion}: The 81.2\% detection rate in 2024 reflects genuine market structure persistence, not loose methodology. Framework correctly identifies persistent regimes when present (2024) and rejects non-regimes when market structure lacks persistence (2020). The temporal coincidence with 0DTE options proliferation (2020 <5\% volume share, 2024 43\% volume share~\cite{cboe2024zero}) suggests a causal relationship, though further analysis is needed to isolate 0DTE effects from other concurrent market changes.

\subsection{Statistical Significance}

Binomial test comparing 2024 vs 2020 detection rates:

\begin{equation}
H_0: p_{2024} = p_{2020} \quad \text{vs} \quad H_1: p_{2024} > p_{2020}
\end{equation}

Result: $p < 0.0001$ (highly significant)

The 69.1 percentage point difference is statistically significant at any reasonable threshold, confirming that 2024 and 2020 represent fundamentally different market regimes.
