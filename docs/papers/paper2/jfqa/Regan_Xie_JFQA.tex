\title{The ``Scar Tissue'' of 0DTE: Identifying Structural Shifts in Dealer Inventory and Overnight Risk Premia}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We document a structural shift in options markets marked by the emergence of persistent dealer gamma regimes. Using a structural reasoning framework (2020--2025), we find dealer positioning transitioned from an exceptional state (12\% detection) to a permanent feature (100\%) in the 0DTE era. We propose a ``scar tissue'' mechanism where intraday hedging leaves residual inventory that market makers carry overnight. This is supported by a ``great divergence'' in SPY returns: since 2023, overnight returns (+21.2\%) outperform intraday returns (+0.6\%), consistent with displaced risk premiums. Our results suggest 0DTE proliferation has reorganized market microstructure, creating persistent regimes of decentralized coordination.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} large language models, regime detection, gamma exposure, market microstructure, structural reasoning, dealer constraints, obfuscation testing, 0DTE options, persistent regimes
