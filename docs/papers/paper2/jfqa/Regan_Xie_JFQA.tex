\title{The ``Scar Tissue'' of 0DTE: Identifying Structural Shifts in Dealer Inventory and Overnight Risk Premia}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We document a fundamental structural shift in options markets characterized by the emergence of persistent dealer gamma regimes. Using a structural reasoning framework applied to 30-day sequences across six years (2020--2025), we find that sustained dealer positioning has transitioned from an exceptional state (12\% detection in 2020) to a permanent characteristic of the post-0DTE era (100\% in 2024). We propose a ``scar tissue'' mechanism where concentrated intraday 0DTE hedging flows leave residual inventory that market makers carry overnight. This is supported by a ``great divergence'' in SPY returns: since late 2023, overnight returns (+21.2\%) have significantly outperformed intraday returns (+0.6\%), consistent with a displacement of risk premium to the overnight session. Our results suggest that 0DTE proliferation has reorganized market microstructure, transforming transient hedging constraints into persistent regimes of decentralized coordination.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} large language models, regime detection, gamma exposure, market microstructure, structural reasoning, dealer constraints, obfuscation testing, 0DTE options, persistent regimes
