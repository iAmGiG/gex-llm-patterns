Table~\ref{tab:cost} summarizes what the three models and their variants cost (the totals of the abstract count the three models only; the variants add 15{,}583 requests and 3.1 summed GPU hours): GPU time, not money, on shared A100 GPUs at no API fee. The GPU hours are the engine time of each run (process seconds, including the start of the engine), summed over the runs of a model. Runs overlapped on several GPUs, so the sum is larger than the elapsed time, which we give as the hours during which at least one engine of the model was running: gpt-oss-20b 10.0 summed and 4.9 elapsed hours on three GPUs, Llama~3.1~8B 3.3 and 2.2 on five, and Qwen3-8B 98.5 and 31.7 on four. Two Qwen3-8B engines shared a GPU at times, and the GPUs were busy for 82.3 of the 98.5 summed hours. Both figures depend on the load that other users put on the GPUs, so they are not a throughput benchmark. The tokens per answer are the better measure of the work: Qwen3-8B writes about three times as many completion tokens as gpt-oss-20b and 32 times as many as Llama~3.1~8B, and gpt-oss-20b about 2.5 times as many as the hosted alias (Paper A). The 14 retries of gpt-oss-20b and the 10{,}022 of Qwen3-8B reached the first limit of 4{,}000 tokens; the second limit left 1{,}032 windows of Qwen3-8B without an answer.
