Table~\ref{tab:cost} summarizes what the three models cost: GPU time, not money, on shared A100 GPUs at no API fee. The GPU hours are engine wall-clock time summed over chunks; they include the start of the engine and depend on the load that other users put on the GPU, so they are not a throughput benchmark. The tokens per answer are the better measure of the work: Qwen3-8B writes about three times as many completion tokens as gpt-oss-20b and 32 times as many as Llama~3.1~8B, and gpt-oss-20b about 2.5 times as many as the hosted alias. The 14 retries of gpt-oss-20b and the 10{,}022 of Qwen3-8B reached the first limit of 4{,}000 tokens; the second limit left 1{,}032 windows of Qwen3-8B without an answer.
