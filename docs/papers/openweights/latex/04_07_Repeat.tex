Each arm carries a sample of windows that was requested twice in the same job (Section~\ref{sec:methods:repeats}). The repeat returns the first verdict in 593 of the 594 windows of gpt-oss-20b, and in all 588 windows of Llama~3.1~8B with the verdict first, whose answers are constant. For Qwen3-8B, 483 of the 588 repeated windows were answered in both passes, and in 480 of these the verdict is the same; the 105 other windows were unanswered in at least one pass (92 in one pass only, 13 in both), so that the retest of Qwen3-8B is conditional on answering, too. Llama~3.1~8B with the reasoning first repeats its verdict in 353 of 588 windows, 55.7\% of the 235 repeated windows of the complete series (kappa 0.113): its verdicts are unstable. A full repeat of the 1{,}476 windows of \ident{bridge_jan} with the hosted alias returned the first verdict in 99.3\% (Paper A). Where they answered, the pinned models gpt-oss-20b and Qwen3-8B were therefore not less repeatable than the hosted one on these samples; whether Qwen3-8B answers at all is not repeatable, and the samples differ (5\% same-job repeats here, a full-arm repeat there), and the figure of Llama~3.1~8B with the verdict first reflects its near-constant answer. The repeats bound the variation within a job; drift between jobs, GPUs or engine versions was not tested.

The sampling defaults differ by model (Table~\ref{tab:models}): gpt-oss-20b samples at temperature 1.0, Llama~3.1~8B at 0.6 with top-p 0.9 and Qwen3-8B at 0.6 with top-p 0.95 and top-k 20. So a difference between the models could come from the defaults rather than from the models. The decoding control of Section~\ref{sec:methods:variants} reruns the published inputs for gpt-oss-20b and for Llama~3.1~8B with the verdict first, with temperature 0 in every request (Table~\ref{tab:decoding}). Temperature 0 changes neither result. gpt-oss-20b answers 1{,}475 of the 1{,}476 windows (24 answers reached 4{,}000 tokens, 23 finished at 8{,}000 and one stays unanswered), agrees with the rule in 99.7\% of them (kappa 0.995), with the stored o4-mini verdicts in 99.7\% of 1{,}411 windows (kappa 0.994) and with the gpt-6-luna arm of Paper A in 99.3\% of 1{,}475 (kappa 0.986), and gives the verdict of its default-sampling arm in 1{,}465 of 1{,}475 windows (99.3\%); its 73 repeats that parsed twice all repeat the verdict. Llama~3.1~8B with the verdict first detects all 1{,}476 windows at temperature 0 as it does at its default sampling (the same verdict on 1{,}476 of 1{,}476 windows), and its 74 repeats are identical. The control covers one arm and two models; Qwen3-8B was not rerun at temperature 0, and the result does not extend to the other arms.
