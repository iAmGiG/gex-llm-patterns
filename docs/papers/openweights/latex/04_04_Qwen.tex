\subsection{Qwen3-8B Follows the Rule Where It Answers, and Leaves Windows Unanswered}
\label{sec:results:qwen}

% src: llm_parse_stats.csv model=qwen3-8b arm_role=primary (n_rows 11,750; n_parsed 10,718; n_finish_length 1,032; mean_completion_tokens 5,368 row-weighted on the seven arms) and gpt-6-luna arms (758, Paper A); cost_ledger.csv model=qwen3-8b (n_requests summed 22,400; first pass 12,378; the retry runs run_metadata.retry_of 10,022); runs.csv.gz run_metadata.local.seconds (summed 354,438.9 s = 98.5 h); missing_answer_bounds.csv period=all arm=qwen_* (n_unparsed summed 1,032, n_unparsed_clf_no_detect summed 1,011; qwen_full_spy parsed_pct_clf_detect 98.850575, parsed_pct_clf_no_detect 88.290054); planning/02_decisions_log.md D-32 (pilot 36 of 40 parsed against the 95 percent gate; amendment before the arms)
Qwen3-8B reasons at great length: its answers average 5{,}368 completion tokens on the seven arms, against 758 for gpt-6-luna on the same arms (Paper A). Of its 12{,}378 first-pass requests, 10{,}022 reached the limit of 4{,}000 tokens and were requested again at 8{,}000, so its arms sent 22{,}400 requests in 98.5 GPU hours. After the retry, 10{,}718 of the 11{,}750 windows of the seven arms (91.2\%) hold a parsed answer; each of the other 1{,}032 (8.8\%) reached 8{,}000 tokens before the JSON object. The 40-window pilot had parsed 36 answers, short of the 95\% that the protocol required; before the arms ran we amended the protocol (decision D-32, disclosed) to run them unchanged and to report such windows as unanswered, so the cap was kept by design after the gate failed, not fixed in advance; every statement about Qwen3-8B below is conditional on its answering.

The unanswered windows are not a random subset. 1{,}011 of the 1{,}032 are windows that the rule rejects: on the complete series Qwen3-8B answers 98.9\% of the windows that the rule detects but 88.3\% of those that it rejects (Table~S6). A rate over the answered windows would therefore overstate the detection rate, and we report bounds: the lower bound counts every unanswered window as not detected and the upper bound as detected (Figure~\ref{fig:rate}).

Where it answers, Qwen3-8B follows the rule: it agrees with the rule in 98.7\% of the answered windows of the complete series (kappa 0.948) and in 99.4\% without same-day expiries (kappa 0.973), and with gpt-6-luna in 98.5\% (kappa 0.941). On the published inputs it gives the verdict of o4-mini in 1{,}273 of the 1{,}287 stored verdicts that it answered (98.9\%, kappa 0.978), and it answers and detects all 501 windows of 2024 and 2025.

% src: missing_answer_bounds.csv period=2024-2025 arm=qwen_full_spy (n_windows 502, n_parsed 399, n_detected_parsed 73, lower_pct 14.541833, upper_pct 35.059761, clf_rate_pct 11.952191) and arm=qwen_ex0dte_spy (465 parsed, 80 detected, 15.936255 to 23.306773, clf 14.741036); stats.yaml rates llm full_spy 2024-2025 (63 of 502; Paper A); stats.yaml contrasts series_contrast family=label qwen_p34_obf_2024 vs qwen_p34_dated_2024 (n_common_windows 190, windows_only 1 and 5, bootstrap_quarter p 0.125); detection_by_year.csv arm=qwen_p34_obf_2024 and qwen_p34_dated_2024 (n_parsed 212 and 197); missing_answer_bounds.csv period=all (parsed counts; qwen_p34_obf_2024 parsed_pct_clf_no_detect 78.571429, qwen_p34_dated_2024 45.238095; lower_pct 82.06278 and 83.856502; qwen_p34_dated_2020 upper_pct 14.349776); disagreements.csv arm=qwen_p34_dated_2024 (9 rows, all llm_detected true, clf_detected false); detection_by_year.csv qwen_p34_dated_2024 kappa 0.667542
The bounds are wide where many windows are unanswered. On the 399 windows of the complete series from 2024 and 2025 that it answered it detects 73 and the rule 57; over all 502 windows, 103 of them unanswered, its rate lies between 14.5\% and 35.1\%, against 12.0\% for the rule and 12.5\% for gpt-6-luna (Paper A); without same-day expiries the bounds are 15.9\% and 23.3\%, against 14.7\% for the rule (Paper A \cite{regan2026rerun}). In the 2024 baseline the dates change nothing that the data can detect: on the 190 windows answered under both labelings it detects 178 with relative labels and 182 with dates (quarter-block bootstrap $p=0.125$). With dates, however, it answers fewer of the windows that the rule rejects (19 of 42, against 33 with relative labels) and detects 9 of the 19, which makes this arm its largest departure from the rule (kappa 0.668). Its 2024-versus-2020 contrast is at least 70.0 percentage points with relative labels and 69.5 with dates (lower bound for 2024 minus upper bound for 2020), so it passes the part of the test that Llama~3.1~8B fails.
