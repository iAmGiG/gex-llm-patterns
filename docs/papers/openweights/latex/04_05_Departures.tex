\subsection{Where the Models Depart from the Rule}
\label{sec:results:departures}

% src: disagreements.csv arm_role=primary, arms bridge_jan, full_spy, ex0dte_spy, p34_* and their oss_ and qwen_ twins (rows per model: gpt-oss-20b 32, qwen3-8b 108, gpt-6-luna 37 on the seven arms); the criterion that the rule's numbers fail (clf_persistence_pct < 70, clf_avg_magnitude_bn < 5, clf_sign_flips > 5): oss 26 flips only, 2 magnitude only, 1 persistence only, 3 model rejects and rule detects; qwen 83, 14, 1, 1 (two criteria), 9; gpt-6-luna 24, 2, 0, 0, 11 (Paper A); figures/output/bf04_departures.json counts
Two reasoning models read the windows much as the rule does, but they do not depart from it at random. In the seven arms Qwen3-8B differs from the rule in 108 windows, and in 99 of them it detects a regime that the rule rejects (Figure~\ref{fig:depart}). In 83 of these 99 the only criterion that the window fails is the limit of five sign flips: the window has six to ten flips and Qwen3-8B reports three to five. Fourteen windows fail only the magnitude criterion, nine of them with a computed mean $|\mathrm{GEX}|$ between \$4.97 and \$5.00 billion, which Qwen3-8B reports as \$4.97 to \$5.00 billion and accepts; one fails only the persistence criterion and one fails two criteria. In the 9 windows that the rule detects and Qwen3-8B does not, it reports a mean $|\mathrm{GEX}|$ below \$5 billion where the computed value is above it in 7.

gpt-oss-20b differs in 32 windows, 29 of them detections that the rule rejects (26 fail only the flip limit, 2 only the magnitude criterion and 1 only the persistence criterion). gpt-6-luna, for reference, differs in 37 windows of the same arms (Paper A), 26 of them detections of rejected windows, 24 of these at the flip limit. All three therefore err towards detection and at the flip limit; Qwen3-8B does so most often, and 52 of its 83 flip-limit windows have six flips, the smallest excess. The counting of sign changes over 30 values is the step on which the answers fail: Qwen3-8B reports the sign-flip count exactly in 87.3\% of its answers on the complete series, against 93.4\% for gpt-6-luna. Its reported mean $|\mathrm{GEX}|$ lies within 5\% of the computed value in 96.9\% to 99.5\% of the answered windows per arm. The windows at the edge of a threshold are the contested windows of Section~\ref{sec:methods:statistics}: on the 521 of the complete series, gpt-oss-20b agrees with the rule on 98.3\% and Qwen3-8B on 92.3\% of the 454 that it answers (Section~\ref{sec:results:qwen}), and gpt-6-luna on 97.5\% (Paper A). A reader who needs the rule's exact verdict on a window at the edge of a threshold should use the rule; the models are not a replacement for it.
