\subsection{gpt-oss-20b Reproduces the Stored Verdicts}
\label{sec:results:oss}

% src: llm_parse_stats.csv model=gpt-oss-20b arm_role=primary (n_rows 11,750, n_parsed 11,748, n_rerun_at_retry_cap 14, n_fallback 1, mean_completion_tokens); bridge_agreement.csv comparison=bridge ref_arm=r1_o4mini scope=all other_arm=oss_bridge_jan (n_pairs 1412; 646, 0, 6, 760; agree_pct 99.575071; kappa 0.991446) and other_arm=bridge_jan (646, 0, 5, 761; kappa 0.992871; cited from Paper A); stats.yaml rates llm oss_bridge_jan 2024-2025 (501 of 501), r1_o4mini 2024-2025 (486 of 486 answered)
gpt-oss-20b, the open-weights model of the developer of gpt-6-luna, sent 12{,}398 requests in 10.0 GPU hours, and 11{,}748 of the 11{,}750 windows of its seven arms hold a parsed answer. On the 1{,}412 windows whose o4-mini verdict is stored it gives the same verdict in 1{,}406 (99.6\%, kappa 0.991); the hosted alias gives it in 1{,}407 (kappa 0.993, from Paper A). It detects all 501 windows of 2024 and 2025 on these inputs, as o4-mini did for the 486 of them that it answered. The published inputs are a fixed set of requests here; Paper A documents what they contain. A model whose weights are fixed at one revision therefore reproduces the stored verdicts of the retired model, and does not need the hosted alias to do so.

% src: detection_by_year.csv label_source=llm scope=all arm=oss_full_spy (agree_pct 99.722755, kappa 0.987793), arm=full_spy (kappa 0.984988); stats.yaml rates llm oss_full_spy 2024-2025 (62 of 502, bootstrap_quarter ci_pct 5.675-19.798), classifier new_full (60 of 502), llm full_spy (63 of 502; Paper A); bridge_agreement.csv comparison=model_pair ref_arm=full_spy other_arm=oss_full_spy scope=all (agree_pct 99.381531, kappa 0.972920; n_disagree_with_clf 29: n_disagree_other_matches_clf 16, n_disagree_ref_matches_clf 13)
On the complete series it agrees with the rule in 99.7\% of the windows (kappa 0.988, against 0.985 for the hosted alias) and detects 62 of the 502 windows of 2024 and 2025 (12.4\%; quarter-block bootstrap 5.7--19.8\%), between the rule (60) and gpt-6-luna (63). It gives the verdict of gpt-6-luna in 99.4\% of the windows of the complete series (kappa 0.973); where the two differ, the rule sides with gpt-oss-20b in 16 and with gpt-6-luna in 13 of 29 windows. Its answers are longer than those of the hosted alias, 1{,}865 completion tokens on average against 758 on the same arms. Fourteen first-pass answers ended at the limit of 4{,}000 tokens and parsed after the retry at 8{,}000. Two windows of \ident{oss_full_spy} have no parsed answer: the answer closes the reasoning field with a typographic quotation mark followed by control tokens of the model, so that no parser recovers the object (Table~S4).

In the 2024 baseline it detects 185 of 223 windows with relative labels and 183 with the dates (exact McNemar test at stride one $p=0.688$; quarter-block bootstrap $p=0.633$), so the model shows no date effect that these data can detect. Table~S4 gives the arms in detail.
