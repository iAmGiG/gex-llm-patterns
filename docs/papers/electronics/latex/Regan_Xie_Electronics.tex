\abstract{Temporal obfuscation tests whether a large language model (LLM) reasons about numbers or recalls market history. Our earlier work reported that an LLM detects persistent dealer-gamma regimes in 30-day windows of obfuscated daily gamma exposure (GEX) of SPY, with a sharp rise in detections after 2023. Here we re-run that test on the complete data. The published 2024--2025 inputs were the call leg of the options chain without the put leg. We rebuilt six daily series, replayed the deterministic rule on them, and bridged o4-mini to gpt-6-luna on the exact published inputs. We also reran the 2024 and 2020 baselines with relative day labels instead of calendar dates. On the published inputs the rule detects 501 of 501 windows (100.0\%) of 2024--2025; on the complete series it detects 60 of 502 (12.0\%), and gpt-6-luna 63 of 502 (12.5\%). The two models agree on 99.6\% of the published windows (kappa 0.993), and gpt-6-luna follows the rule in 99.7\% of 4,671 windows (kappa 0.986). Removing same-day expiries gives 14.7\%, not the published rate. With relative labels gpt-6-luna detects 80.7\% of the 2024 baseline windows, against 81.2\% with dates. The complete series shows no post-2023 increase that the data distinguish from zero.}
