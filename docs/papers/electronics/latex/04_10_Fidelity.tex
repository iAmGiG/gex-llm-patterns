\subsection{Model Fidelity to the Rule}
\label{sec:results:fidelity}

Table~\ref{tab:fidelity} and Figure~\ref{fig:fidelity} compare each model answer with the deterministic classifier on the same window.
% src: detection_by_year.csv label_source=llm scope=all (n_compared, agree_pct, kappa, metrics_agree_pct, sign_flips_exact_pct); disagreements.csv (60 rows of the gpt-6-luna arms; the oss_* rows belong to Section 4.12; causes derived from llm_sign_flips, clf_sign_flips, llm_avg_magnitude_bn, clf_avg_magnitude_bn); figures/output/fig07_llm_classifier_fidelity.json extra.groups and extra.borderline
The verdict of the model equals the verdict of the rule in 99.6\% or more of the windows of every SPY arm (99.7\% on \ident{full_spy}, 99.7\% on \ident{ex0dte_spy}), with kappa between 0.984 and 0.995 on the four long SPY arms (\ident{bridge_jan}, \ident{bridge_old}, \ident{full_spy}, \ident{ex0dte_spy}). The published o4-mini answers equal the rule in all 1{,}412 windows, and gpt-6-luna reaches 99.4\% and 99.7\% on the scaled QQQ and IWM series. On the unscaled QQQ and IWM arms the accuracy is 100.0\% and kappa is undefined, because the rule labels no window as persistent. Over the answers drawn in panels (c) to (e) of Figure~\ref{fig:fidelity} (33{,}228 parsed answers) the verdict equals the rule's in 99.8\% of the 31{,}752 gpt-6-luna answers and in 100.0\% of the 1{,}476 o4-mini answers.

The reported metrics are less exact than the verdict, and least exact on the unscaled QQQ and IWM arms (82.4\% to 89.4\%). The share of answers whose reported counts, magnitude and persistence equal the computed values (within 0.5 days, \$0.05 billion and 0.5 percentage points) is 99.4\% for o4-mini and between 92.7\% and 98.1\% for gpt-6-luna on the four long SPY arms. The sign-flip count is exact in 100.0\% of the o4-mini answers and in 93.4\% to 95.6\% of the gpt-6-luna answers on the four long SPY arms (93.4\% over all gpt-6-luna answers of Figure~\ref{fig:fidelity}); the model undercounts flips in 6.0\% of its answers and overcounts them in 0.6\%. Its reported persistence lies within the tolerance of the figure (0.5 percentage points) in 95.0\% of the answers and its reported magnitude within \$0.05 billion in 98.7\%, against 99.9\% and 99.6\% for o4-mini. The median ratio of reported to computed magnitude is 1.000.

The few verdicts that differ from the rule lie at the thresholds. Seven gpt-6-luna arms have windows in which the verdict differs from the rule (\ident{full_spy}, \ident{ex0dte_spy}, \ident{full_qqq_scaled}, \ident{bridge_old}, \ident{bridge_jan}, \ident{full_iwm_scaled}, \ident{p34_obf_2024}). They hold 60 windows in all, listed in the result files of the repository (Appendix~\ref{app:data}), with four causes:
\begin{itemize}
\item 41 windows in which the model reports at most five sign flips where the rule counts six or more, so that it calls a window persistent that the rule classifies as low conviction;
\item 15 windows in which the reported mean magnitude lies on the other side of \$5 billion than the computed one;
\item 2 windows with overcounted flips;
\item 2 windows whose reported values are correct and meet all three criteria but which the model labels transitional (persistence 70.0\% and 73.3\%, close to the 70\% threshold).
\end{itemize}
In 43 of the 60 windows the model detects a regime that the rule rejects, and in 17 it rejects one that the rule detects. In 52 of the 60 windows the computed values lie in the borderline region of Figure~\ref{fig:fidelity}, that is a persistence within one day of 21 of 30 days (66.7--73.3\%), a mean magnitude between \$4.50 and \$5.50 billion or 4 to 6 flips. Away from these regions the model reproduces the rule.
