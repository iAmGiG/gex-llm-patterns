\subsection{Calendars, Flagged Windows and the Table of Windows}
\label{sup:detail:windows}

A span that covers a calendar gap of more than four days is flagged (a gap of five or more days is not a holiday weekend). The exchange closure of 2012-10-29 and 2012-10-30 (Hurricane Sandy) is such a gap without a missing trading day, so in each series that covers 2012 (\ident{new_full} and \ident{new_ex0dte} for the three symbols, and \ident{old_bs}) the 29 windows that span it are flagged although they are complete. The number of trading days of the symbol's master calendar (\path{gex.options_daily_summary}) that are absent from the series inside the span is stored with the window. A third detector compares the span with the NYSE trading calendar and stores the number of NYSE trading days that the series lacks inside it (\ident{nyse_missing_days}); it finds a single day that is absent from the series and from the master calendar alike, which the first two detectors cannot see. A flag marks a window but does not drop it, and flagged windows are kept in every rate.
% src: condensed/windows.csv.gz series_key=new_full (window_end of the windows with nyse_missing_days > 0: 2018-11, 2018-12, 2019-09, 2019-10, 2020-06 to 2020-08 for SPY, plus 2024-10 and 2024-11 for QQQ); series parquet new_full_SPY (calendar gaps above four days: 2012-10-26 to 2012-10-31, 2019-08-30 to 2019-09-04)
The gap detector fires on two spans of the complete series, the closure of 2012-10-29 and 2012-10-30, when no day is missing, and the absence of 2019-09-03; the NYSE detector fires on 2018-11-06, 2019-09-03 and 2020-06-23 for all three symbols and on 2024-09-30 for QQQ.

% src: series parquet new_full_SPY against old_bs_SPY (trading_date): 53 days of new_full that old_bs lacks = 19 (spot-filled days of 2009 to 2018, series_metadata spot_fill.days) + 2 (2023-07-12, 2024-03-11) + 26 (2026-01-02 to 2026-02-09) + 1 (2026-05-06) + 5 (2026-10-05 to 2026-10-09)
Windows use the rows of each series (rule A, Section~3.4 of the article), so the calendars matter. The series \ident{old_bs} lacks 53 trading days of the complete series for SPY: the 19 days of 2009 to 2018 and the day 2026-05-06 that were added to the daily table on 2026-11-03 (Section~3.3 of the article), 2023-07-12, 2024-03-11, the 26 trading days from 2026-01-02 to 2026-02-09, and the 5 days from 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-09. The January calendar lacks 2023-07-12 and 2024-03-11; both dates are in the complete series. For the intraday series, the \ident{market_close} slot (20:00:59 UTC) equals the end-of-day row on the 51 regular days, so the independent late-session observation is \ident{final_rush}. The irregular days 2026-07-29, 2026-08-03 and 2026-09-14 are excluded (Section~S4 of the Supplementary Materials).

The composite intraday windows (rule T0, key \ident{intraday_t0}) take the \ident{new_full} values for days $T{-}29$ to $T{-}1$ and the \ident{final_rush} value at $T{+}0$. A day without a finite value is left out of a rebuilt series before the windows are cut, and stops the window build for the recovered January and Phase 3 and 4 inputs, which must stay as they were sent; no stored series has such a day, so no window holds a missing value.
