\subsection{Research Questions}
\label{sec:introduction:rqs}

% TODO(intro/rqs): refine the wording with the co-author; keep one question per results subsection.
We address seven questions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=RQ\arabic*.,leftmargin=*]
\item Which daily series were the published inputs, and how do the deterministic labels change on the complete series?
\item Does gpt-6-luna reproduce the o4-mini labels on the exact published inputs?
\item Does the model's regime call follow the input series and the stated criteria?
\item How much of the persistence signal lives in same-day-expiry (0DTE) gamma?
\item Do the answers hold for 2008--2019, for QQQ and IWM, and in the post-publication period (2026)?
\item Does a late-session intraday snapshot reproduce the end-of-day label?
\item Does the 2024-versus-2020 contrast of the earlier baseline runs, whose prompts carried calendar dates, persist when the dates are replaced by relative day labels?
\end{enumerate}

This article makes six contributions: (i)~a reconstruction of the published inputs and an account of the data defect they carried, with the deterministic labels recomputed on the complete series (Section~\ref{sec:results:inputs}); (ii)~a convention-sensitivity analysis of the deterministic regime labels across six daily series (one of them a call-only diagnostic), three assets and 2008--2026 (Sections~\ref{sec:results:replay} and~\ref{sec:results:ablation}--\ref{sec:results:cross}); (iii)~a model-agreement bridge between o4-mini and gpt-6-luna on identical inputs (Sections~\ref{sec:results:bridge} and~\ref{sec:results:corrected}); (iv)~overlap-aware statistics for stride-one windows (Section~\ref{sec:methods:statistics}); (v)~storage of every input, prompt and response in result tables, from which each table and figure of this article is generated (Section~\ref{sec:methods:storage}); and (vi)~an obfuscated-versus-dated rerun of the Phase 3 and 4 baselines, which separates the effect of visible calendar dates from the effect of the model and of the input values (Section~\ref{sec:results:dated}).

\subsection{Organization}
\label{sec:introduction:organization}

Section~\ref{sec:background} reviews dealer gamma exposure, the validation of LLM reasoning by obfuscation and the construct validity of financial inputs. Section~\ref{sec:methods} defines the daily series, the windows and classifier, the model protocol, the experimental arms and the statistics. Section~\ref{sec:results} reports the results, from the lineage of the published inputs to the rerun of the earlier baselines. Section~\ref{sec:discussion} discusses what survives, what the model tracks, the threats to validity and the implications. Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes, and the appendices give the prompt, schema and API configuration (Appendix~\ref{app:prompt}), the series definitions in SQL (Appendix~\ref{app:series}) and the per-year tables (Appendix~\ref{app:tables}).
