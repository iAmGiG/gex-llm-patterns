\subsection{Post-Publication Period}
\label{sec:results:post}

Table~\ref{tab:post} and Figure~\ref{fig:post} cover the post-publication period of Section~\ref{sec:methods:windows}: the windows that end in 2026, of which those with ends from 2026-02-13 lie entirely after the end of the published sample (2025-12-31). We use this label rather than out-of-sample, because gpt-6-luna was created on 2026-09-14 and its training cutoff is undocumented.
% src: dates 2026-09-14 (gpt-6-luna creation; README line 216, runs.model_created_at) and 2026-10-12 (Alpha Vantage to Cboe switch; decisions log line 207, README line 126); post_publication_2026_summary.csv (SPY; definitions ends_in_2026 and entirely_2026; series new_full, new_ex0dte, call_only, new_full_scaled); post_publication_2026.csv (clf_regime_type of detected windows, clf_avg_magnitude_bn); stats.yaml rates period 2026
The period has 194 windows with ends from 2026-01-02 to 2026-10-09, of which 165 lie entirely in 2026 (ends from 2026-02-13) and 29 still contain days of 2025. The last window end is 2026-10-09 because equity options moved from Alpha Vantage to Cboe delayed quotes on 2026-10-12 (Section~\ref{sec:methods:lineage}). On the complete series the rule detects 22 of 194 (11.3\%) of the windows that end in 2026; without same-day expiries it detects 41 of 194 (21.1\%) (Table~\ref{tab:post} also gives the 165 windows entirely in 2026). All detected windows of the complete series are persistent negative-GEX windows, with ends between 2026-03-20 and 2026-04-21. These rates are close to the 12.0\% of 2024 and 2025 on the complete series and far from the 100\% that the published inputs gave for those years. The Wilson interval for the windows ending in 2026 is 7.6--16.6\% (quarter-block bootstrap 0.0--21.8\%, 4 blocks). A call-only series would label 194 of the 194 windows as persistent, with a mean $|\mathrm{GEX}|$ of \$21.74~billion. gpt-6-luna agrees with the rule in 100.0\% of the windows on \ident{new_full} and 100.0\% on \ident{new_ex0dte} (0 and 0 windows of disagreement, respectively). For QQQ and IWM the unscaled series give no detection by the rule or by the model (0 of 194 windows each). On the scaled series the rule detects 16.0\% (QQQ) and 44.8\% (IWM) of the windows ending in 2026 and the model 16.5\% and 45.4\%. The training data of gpt-6-luna may overlap this period, so the agreement of the model with the rule here shows consistency with the rule and says nothing about forecasting.
