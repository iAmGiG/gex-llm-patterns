\section{Per-Year Tables}
\label{sup:tables}

Tables~\ref{tab:app:series}--\ref{tab:complete} give the numbers behind the figures of Section~4 of the article for every year. The years are the calendar years of the window end, and rates are percentages of parsed windows. The Wilson intervals treat overlapping windows as independent and are optimistic; the quarter-block bootstrap interval of every rate is in the result files of the repository (Appendix~A of the article).
