We re-ran the detection of persistent dealer-gamma regimes on complete inputs, that is, on daily series with both the call and the put leg. On the published inputs, which held the call leg only from 2023-09-29, the rule detects every window of 2024 and 2025. On the complete series it detects 12.0\% (60 of 502 windows). The earlier saturation is therefore a property of the inputs, and the regime label is sensitive to the input convention. At the nominal rule the complete series gives no evidence of a rise after 2023 (a change of $-$10.2 points over all windows, 60-window moving-block interval $-$20.9 to 8.5); the interval includes a rise of 5 points or more, so the data neither show a rise nor rule one out, and they are not evidence that the two periods are equal.

A second model, gpt-6-luna, agrees with the published o4-mini answers on 99.6\% of 1{,}412 windows (kappa 0.993) and follows the rule on the complete series in 99.7\% of the windows. These arms test whether a model applies criteria that its prompt states. They cannot separate recall from reasoning, because the rule is in the prompt and is the reference label, and we draw no conclusion on that distinction. Dated and relative day labels give no detectable difference (1 of 223 windows differs in the 2024 baseline), in a comparison that has no positive control. As stated in Section~\ref{sec:introduction:supersedes}, this article supersedes the 2024 and 2025 detection rates of the two earlier publications; no correction has yet been filed with the publishers.

Same-day expiries carry a large part of the absolute net GEX in 2024 to 2026, but removing them does not restore the published rates (14.7\% against 12.0\%). The label is a GEX label defined by three thresholds, not a demonstrated price regime, and the thresholds are fixed in dollars, the dealer side is imputed, and the windows overlap. The complete series is not validated against an independent source. The cross-asset replication and the intraday check are in the Supplementary Materials and are checks, not measurements.

Section~S9 of the Supplementary Materials shows that the rate of 2024 and 2025 depends on the weight of the put leg (17.9\% at 0.9, 12.0\% at 1). Follow-up work includes a variant with symbol-specific and price-adjusted thresholds in the prompt (a new prompt version), a test of the price behaviour of the labelled windows that accounts for the dependence of GEX on the price path, and standards for versioned inputs in LLM validation studies. A companion manuscript \cite{regan2026openweights} repeats seven of the arms with open-weights models on local hardware.
