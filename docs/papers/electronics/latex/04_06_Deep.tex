\subsection{Deep Baseline, 2008--2019}
\label{sec:results:deep}

Figure~\ref{fig:deep} extends the analysis to 2008--2019, before the growth of weekly and daily expirations.
% src: detection_by_year.csv (classifier and llm rows, series new_full, new_ex0dte, old_bs, years 2008-2019); stats.yaml rates period 2008-2019; ex0dte_decomposition.csv (diff_pp, llm_diff_pp, abs_0dte_share_pct); stats.yaml contrasts series_contrast new_full vs new_ex0dte period 2008-2019 (classifier: windows_only_a 120, windows_only_b 10, diff_pp 3.67893, nonoverlap_30d.p_mcnemar_exact 0.375; llm: 124 and 12); detection_by_year.csv classifier rows SPY 2008-2014 (max_mean_abs_gex_bn: largest before 2014 3.906766 new_full, 3.648529 new_ex0dte, 4.026031 old_bs; n_shape_ok summed over 2008-2013: 772 of 1,482 on new_full) and report.yaml diagnostics.floor_years; windows parquet new_full_SPY (clf_negative_days >= 21 in 703 of the 772)
Before 2014 no window of any of the three series is detected, by the rule or by gpt-6-luna; this includes the 476 windows that end in the financial crisis of 2008 and 2009. No window of these years reaches the magnitude criterion: the largest 30-day mean $|\mathrm{GEX}|$ is \$3.91~billion on \ident{new_full}, \$3.65~billion on \ident{new_ex0dte} and \$4.03~billion on the archive. The zero is therefore a floor effect of the fixed dollar threshold, as for QQQ and IWM (Section~\ref{sec:results:cross}), and not an absence of persistent signs: 772 of the 1{,}482 windows of \ident{new_full} that end in 2008 to 2013 (52.1\%) meet the persistence and stability criteria, 703 of them with a negative sign, and fail on magnitude alone. Detections start in 2014, the first year in which a window mean reaches \$5~billion (9.1\% on \ident{new_full}), and peak in 2018 on both complete series, at 34.8\% on \ident{new_full} (87 of 250 windows) and 24.0\% on \ident{new_ex0dte}, the year of the 2018 volatility episode; the archive peaks in 2017 (38.8\%). Pooled over 2008 to 2019 the rule detects 10.2\% of the windows of \ident{new_full} (304 of 2{,}990; Wilson 9.1--11.3\%, quarter-block bootstrap 4.9--16.7\%), 6.5\% of those of \ident{new_ex0dte} and 9.8\% of those of the archive. The model gives 10.2\%, 6.4\% and 9.8\% on the same three series.

% src: detection_by_year.csv label_source=classifier scope=year series_key=new_full, symbols SPY, QQQ (from 2011) and IWM (n_shape_ok and n summed over 2008-2013, 2014-2019, 2020-2023, 2024-2025 and 2026; detection rate equal to the share in 2023-2026 for SPY); series parquet new_full_SPY spot (yearly mean 94.98 in 2009, 724.03 in 2026; squared ratio 58.1)
The fixed threshold is not constant in real terms. GEX grows with the square of the price (Equation~(\ref{eq:gex})), and the mean price of SPY rose from \$95 in 2009 to \$724 in 2026, so that factor alone grew 58-fold, besides the growth of open interest. Without the magnitude criterion, the share of windows of \ident{new_full} with a persistent sign falls over the whole period: for SPY from 52.1\% in 2008 to 2013 to 41.9\% in 2014 to 2019, 28.8\% in 2020 to 2023 and 12.0\% in 2024 and 2025 (11.3\% in 2026), and in the same direction for QQQ (51.0\% in 2011 to 2013, 13.4\% in 2024 and 2025) and IWM (80.0\% and 35.7\%). From 2023 on the magnitude criterion does not bind for SPY, so the share equals the detection rate and the comparison of Section~\ref{sec:results:replay} is unaffected.

The gap between \ident{new_full} and \ident{new_ex0dte} is a consistency check of the ablation arm. It is zero from 2008 to 2013, where neither series reaches the magnitude criterion, so these years cannot test the ablation, and it is positive in every year from 2014 to 2019: 0.8 percentage points in 2014, 6.3 in 2015, up to 12.7 in 2017 and 6.8 in 2019, while the share rises to 22.1\%. The gap is therefore not proportional to the share. The model reproduces it (up to 12.7 percentage points). Over 2008--2019 the complete series detects 3.7 percentage points more than the ex-0DTE series (120 windows detected only with same-day expiries against 10 only without; quarter-block bootstrap $p=5.0\times10^{-4}$; McNemar on the non-overlapping subsample of 100 windows $p=0.375$). Only the bootstrap separates the two series in the decade before 0DTE; the exact test on the non-overlapping subsample, with 4 against 1 discordant windows, cannot ($p=0.375$), whereas the sign of the difference is reversed, without a significant difference, in 2024 and 2025 (Section~\ref{sec:results:ablation}).
