\subsection{Deep Baseline, 2008--2019}
\label{sec:results:deep}

Figure~\ref{fig:deep} extends the analysis to 2008--2019, before the growth of weekly and daily expirations.
% src: detection_by_year.csv (classifier and llm rows, series new_full, new_ex0dte, old_bs, years 2008-2019); stats.yaml rates period 2008-2019; ex0dte_decomposition.csv (diff_pp, llm_diff_pp, abs_0dte_share_pct); stats.yaml contrasts series_contrast new_full vs new_ex0dte period 2008-2019 (classifier: windows_only_a 120, windows_only_b 10, diff_pp 3.67893, nonoverlap_30d.p_mcnemar_exact 0.375; llm: 124 and 12)
Before 2014 no window of any of the three series is detected, by the rule or by gpt-6-luna; this includes the 476 windows that end in the financial crisis of 2008 and 2009. Detections start in 2014 (9.1\% on \ident{new_full}) and peak in 2018 on both complete series, at 34.8\% on \ident{new_full} (87 of 250 windows) and 24.0\% on \ident{new_ex0dte}, the year of the 2018 volatility episode; the archive peaks in 2017 (38.8\%). Pooled over 2008 to 2019 the rule detects 10.2\% of the windows of \ident{new_full} (304 of 2{,}990; Wilson 9.1--11.3\%, quarter-block bootstrap 4.9--16.7\%), 6.5\% of those of \ident{new_ex0dte} and 9.8\% of those of the archive. The model gives 10.2\%, 6.4\% and 9.8\% on the same three series.

The gap between \ident{new_full} and \ident{new_ex0dte} is a consistency check of the ablation arm. It is zero from 2008 to 2013, although same-day expiries carry 11.5\% of the absolute net GEX in 2013, and it is positive in every year from 2014 to 2019: 0.8 percentage points in 2014, 6.3 in 2015, up to 12.7 in 2017 and 6.8 in 2019, while the share rises to 22.1\%. The gap is therefore not proportional to the share. The model reproduces it (up to 12.7 percentage points). Over 2008--2019 the complete series detects 3.7 percentage points more than the ex-0DTE series (120 windows detected only with same-day expiries against 10 only without; quarter-block bootstrap $p=5.0\times10^{-4}$; McNemar on the non-overlapping subsample of 100 windows $p=0.375$). Only the bootstrap separates the two series in the decade before 0DTE; the exact test on the non-overlapping subsample, with 4 against 1 discordant windows, cannot ($p=0.375$), whereas the sign of the difference is reversed, without a significant difference, in 2024 and 2025 (Section~\ref{sec:results:ablation}).
